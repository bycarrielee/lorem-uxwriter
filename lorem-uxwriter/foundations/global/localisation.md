# Localisation standards 
Guidelines for writing translation-ready content. Assume our products will be localised and write accordingly. All content must meet these standards. 

## 1. Allow for text expansion
Translations may be longer. When designing or setting character limits, account for expansion.
- Chinese: -25% average
- Malay: +15% average
- Tamil: +40% average

## 2. Frontload information
- Translated copy may be truncated. Always start your sentence with the most important information. 

## 3. Avoid idioms, puns, and jokes
- Idioms cannot be translated word for word as they are dependent on culture and context.
- Puns and joke rarely translate. Humour also differ across cultures.

| Bad example                           | Good example                        |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| `Let's get the ball rolling`<br>      | `Let's start`                       |
| `Your application is in the pipeline` | `We're processing your application` |
## 4. Keep text and images separate
- Avoid embedding text in images. 
- If unavoidable, provide localised versions.

## 5. Add text labels to icons 
- Icons may have different meanings in different cultures.

## 6. Do not use UI elements in a sentence
- For example: form fields, buttons, icons.
- Layout may change in translation. Translators may also need to translate text without looking at the UI.

| Bad example                                                                         | Good example                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| `I'm living on the `[FORM FIELD]` floor, and my unit number is `[FORM FIELD]`.`<br> | ```Floor and unit number[FORM FIELD]``` |
| `Select [DROPDOWN] to choose your country`                                          | `Country: [DROPDOWN]`                   |

---
 If translating, search https://www.translatedterms.gov.sg for official terms in Chinese, Malay, and Tamil.
