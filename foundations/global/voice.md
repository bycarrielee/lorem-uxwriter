# Voice guidelines
Default voice guidelines for products without a custom voice. Products can override by creating `foundations/{product}/voice.md`. 

Voice is the consistent personality of the product. It does not change. All content must align with the defined voice attributes.

## Voice attributes
We sound like a knowledgeable civil servant who respects your time, tells you exactly what you need to know, and never makes things harder than they need to be.

### 1. Clear, not bureaucratic
- Use conversational plain language that's easy to understand.
- No jargon and legalistic language.
- No unnecessary complexity.

### Examples

| Bad example                                                                                                                                                      | Good example                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `Applications must be duly submitted prior to the aforementioned deadline, failing which the applicant shall be required to reinitiate the application process.` | `You need to submit your application by 15 March 2026. If you miss this date, you'll need to reapply.` |
| `Furnish photographic documentation of your NRIC in its entirety.`                                                                                               | `Upload a photo of your NRIC (front and back).`                                                        |

### 2. Helpful, not condescending

- Be supportive, patient, and respectful of all literacy and skill levels.
- Offer additional help where necessary, but do not over-explain.
- Do not talk down to users or make assumptions about them.

### Examples
| Bad example                                                                                                                                           | Good example                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `"Don't worry if this seems confusing! We know forms can be tricky. Just pick whichever one you think might be right—you can always change it later!` | `Select the holding type for your HDB flat. If you're not sure, log in to MY HDBPage to check.` |
| `This is very important, so please pay close attention: you really need to make absolutely sure that everything is correct...`                        | `Check your answers before you submit. You can't change them after.`                            |

### 3. Direct but empathetic

- Be clear about rules and decisions. If appropriate, you can acknowledge when they are difficult.
- Do not apologise for policy, routine system behaviour, or things beyond our control.
- Do not be cold, robotic, or over-emotional.

| Bad example                                                                                                                                       | Good example                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Application rejected. Household income exceeds threshold.`<br>                                                                                   | `You don't qualify because your household income is $4,200. The limit is $3,000. You can reapply if your income changes.` |
| `We're so deeply sorry for your tragic loss. We can't imagine how devastated you must be. Take all the time you need—there's absolutely no rush.` | `Losing a loved one is difficult. Here's what you need to do next: update their records by 30 April 2026.`                |

### 4. Transparent, not evasive

- Be upfront about requirements, timelines, decisions, and next steps.
- Do not hide information or be vague about processes.
- Do not bury important details. Front-load them.

| Bad example                                                                    | Good example                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Your application is being processed. You will be notified in due course.`<br> | `We'll process your application within 14 working days. You'll get an email when we've made a decision.`                                          |
| `Please provide relevant income information as applicable.`                    | `You need to declare all household income, including part-time work. If you don't, your application will be rejected and you may face penalties.` |

## Tone adjustments

Our tone may adapt to context. 

| Context             | Tone                        | Example                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Success             | Confirming, brief           | `Application submitted. We'll email you within 14 days.`                                                                                      |
| Neutral             | Clear, direct               | `Enter your postal code.`                                                                                                                     |
| Guidance            | Helpful, specific           | `Upload a JPG or PNG file. Maximum size: 5 MB.`                                                                                               |
| Warning             | Clear, no panic             | `You have 3 days left to submit. After that, you'll need to restart your application.                                                         |
| Error               | Specific, solution-focused  | `Invalid mobile number. Mobile numbers must start with 8 or 9 and be 8 digits long.`                                                          |
| Rejection           | Direct, factual, actionable | `Your application does not meet the eligibility criteria. Your household income is $4,200. The limit is $3,000.`                              |
| Destructive actions | Serious, precise            | `This will permanently delete your account and all associated data, including your profile, history, and saved items. This cannot be undone.` |

### When to be warmer
- First-time users completing their profile
- Successfully completing complex multi-step processes
- Acknowledging difficult circumstances (hardship applications, appeals)

### When to stay neutral
- Transactional steps (form filling, document upload)
- Status updates (application received, being processed)
- Technical instructions
- Reference information and FAQs

### When to be more firm and direct
- Eligibility criteria and requirements (no room for ambiguity)
- Deadlines and consequences
- Compliance and legal obligations
- Fraud warnings and penalties
- Irreversible actions (withdrawal, cancellation)

# Writing principles

**Be concise**
- Shorter is better
- One idea per sentence
- Remove unnecessary words

**Be specific**
- "Your file is too large" not "Invalid file"
- "Enter your email address" not "Enter information"

**Be positive**
- Focus on what users can do
- "To continue, sign in" not "You can't access this without signing in"

**Be direct**
- Get to the point quickly
- No preamble or throat-clearing
- Start with the most important information
